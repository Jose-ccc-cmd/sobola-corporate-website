Student attendance and school gate records

Gate & Attendance helps a school organize student entry, attendance, approved pass-outs, and exit records. It is a digital school gate management and access-control workflow for checkpoint events, not GPS location tracking or a complete school management system. Schools can ask Sobola Finance Ltd about setup, reporting, and a demonstration of this school attendance monitoring solution in Uganda.

Product information

School gate and attendance questions

What school attendance information does it record?

Gate & Attendance is designed to record student arrivals, attendance at configured checkpoints, approved pass-outs, and exits. Schools can ask Sobola about attendance reports, notifications, and how the workflow fits their existing registers.

Does it include RFID, visitor management, or GPS tracking?

The website describes wristband-based checkpoint checks but does not confirm RFID or NFC hardware, GPS tracking, or a visitor-management module. Schools should confirm those requirements, device compatibility, and any integrations with Sobola before selecting a school access-control system.

Does Sobola offer a school meal-planning system?

Sobola's published product information covers Gate & Attendance and Pocket Pay. It does not list a separate school meal-planning or student meal-management product, and no integration with meal software is confirmed here. Contact the team to discuss specific school requirements.
