Our purpose

Make essential services easier to manage.

Many schools and institutions still rely on cash, paper registers and separate reporting processes. These manual workflows can make record keeping, reconciliation and accountability harder.

Sobola develops digital tools that help organise these processes through organised records, timely transaction visibility, reporting tools and straightforward management interfaces. Technology should be practical and shaped around the people who use it.

Pocket Pay

Digital pocket money for school communities.

Pocket Pay helps schools, parents, students and authorised school service providers manage student pocket money. Parents or guardians can add funds to a learner's account; students can use an assigned wristband to make authorised purchases at supported school points.

Each supported wristband purchase is recorded electronically, helping parents and authorised school teams review account activity. Canteen and school-shop records can support reporting, sales monitoring, account reviews and reconciliation. Payment channels are provided through the school's configured setup and relevant service providers.

School operations

Connected tools for school operations.

Attendance reporting

Capture and organise student attendance records digitally. Structured information helps authorised administrators review attendance and prepare reports.

Gate and security

Record student entry, approved access and exit events to help schools maintain clearer movement records and accountability at checkpoints.

Meal planning

Digital meal-planning and meal-management tools help institutions organise student meal information, estimate requirements, monitor service and reduce avoidable waste.

Solutions can be used independently or as part of a connected digital environment, based on each institution's needs.

Our approach

Built around the people who use them.

We learn about each institution's processes, users, reporting needs and available infrastructure. Configuration, testing, implementation, user training and technical support can help each tool fit the way the institution works.

Security and accountability

Clear records. Thoughtful access.

Sobola solutions use role-based access so users can work with the information relevant to their responsibilities. Depending on the product, authorised users can review transaction references, activity records, attendance, student information and reports.

Electronic records support traceability and help authorised teams review operational issues. We encourage institutions to manage access responsibly and follow applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

Who we serve

For schools and their communities.

Sobola solutions are designed for primary and secondary schools, boarding and day schools, canteens, cafeterias, school shops, parents, guardians, students, administrators, bursars and institutional service providers.

Each institution has different operating needs. Our goal is to provide technology that is understandable, manageable and suited to its users.

Our experience

Learning and improving since 2024.

Since beginning operations in 2024, we have developed our technology, improved our processes and learned from the day-to-day needs of schools, parents, students, administrators and service operators.

User feedback and responsible partnerships help guide product improvements, technical support and the development of new digital tools.
